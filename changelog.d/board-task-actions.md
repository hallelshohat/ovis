### Added
- A workspace that is a herdr-projects board task's space has the board's actions in its row menu
  (right-click on a desktop, ⋯ on a phone): Archive task, with Undo; Stop agent while it works;
  Approve plan, or Approve and merge after a question; and Remove task after a question that names
  it and says unsaved or unmerged work is kept. A repository's row archives its done tasks, or
  every repo's, at once. The board's own CLI does each action, and its message or refusal shows
  under the workspaces.
